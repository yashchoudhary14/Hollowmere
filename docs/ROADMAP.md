## M1 — Single-player greybox of the full match

One player can play a complete match, from arrival to results, in a greybox village made of primitives. Everything is generated by builders.

**World builder** (`Hollowmere/Build Greybox Village` → `Assets/Hollowmere/Scenes/Greybox.unity`)

- [x] Ground, wall ring, village gate in the north wall, fog road beyond it to the bridge [The village map].
- [x] 15 houses from `HouseCatalog` with visible traits: door colour, chimney count, family nameplate, stopped clock beside the door. Each house has 3 rooms, a hearth, room lights, a fuse box, a wardrobe hiding spot, 3 note spots, and its puzzle rig and part container [Houses and puzzles].
- [x] Town square: altar with 6 slots, stone circle with 6 candles, notice board, bell tower. Chapel with the chained Ritual Book.
- [x] Shrine diamond marker above every house door, hidden by default.
- [x] Roam points on the streets, forest-edge spawn points, NavMeshSurface bake (door panels excluded and carving).
- [x] Sun and moon lights, player, Hollow placeholder. Scene added to Build Settings.

**Player** [Player systems]

- [x] First-person controller: walk 2.5 m/s, crouch 1.5, sprint 5 for 6 s with recovery. Footstep noise radii 0 / 5 / 25 m.
- [x] Interaction raycast with tap and hold prompts.
- [x] Inventory: 3 hand slots, a ritual-part slot (no sprinting while it's full), matches, batteries, keepsake.
- [x] Flashlight with battery drain and flicker when low.
- [x] Microphone loudness to noise with `NoiseSystem.VoiceRadius`, toggled with M.

**World objects**

- [x] Doors: holding E opens slowly and quietly, a quick open creaks (DESIGN.md "Player systems"). Latching at dusk.
- [x] Readables: notes, letters on the notice board, the Ritual Book.
- [x] Fuse box and room lights. Hearth (lit or cold) with a safe zone.
- [x] Wardrobe hiding with hold-breath (Space, 8 s, then a gasp).

**Match flow** [Core loop, Dusk, Night phase]

- [x] `MatchDirector`: Arrival → Day (12 min) → Dusk (90 s, 12 tolls) → Night acts of 6, 8 and 6 min → dawn loss. Includes the midnight bell, unlatching doors in Act 2, and the minute-17 power cut.
- [x] Applies the `MatchPlan`: allotted houses, shrines, hearths, letters, Ritual Book text, puzzle answers.
- [x] Lighting and fog per phase.
- [x] Debug: time-scale field, simulated player count (one person plays several houses), seed field.

**Puzzles** [Houses and puzzles]

- [x] Code lock (4 dials), clock (set the hour, wind with the winding key), sequence (6 symbols, 4 in order). Answers come from `MatchPlan.Solutions`, with hints on the house's notes.
- [x] A solved puzzle opens the container and frees the ritual part.

**Ritual and escape** [Escape]

- [x] Altar slots follow `AltarOrder`. A wrong slot ejects the part, snuffs every candle and makes a loud noise.
- [x] Chant: all parts placed and every living player in the circle starts it. It lasts 60 s; candles can be relit with matches; it resets when every candle is out and pauses when someone steps out.
- [x] Gate opens, then the Crossing: walking only; looking back more than 120° for 0.5 s means you're taken; reaching the bridge means you've escaped.
- [x] Results screen, and restart.

**The Hollow v1** [The monster]

- [x] NavMeshAgent with states Dormant, Roam, Investigate, TestHouse, Hunt, Grab, Ritual, Banished.
- [x] Hearing through `NoiseSystem`, with wall occlusion (40 m outdoors, about 15 m through walls). Sight 20 m, needing light or a target within 3 m.
- [x] Entry rules by act. Lit-hearth safe zones. Grab, then death after 5 s.
- [x] Snuffs candles during the chant. Banished when the gate opens.

**HUD and debug**

- [x] OnGUI HUD: crosshair, prompt and hold bar, phase and timer (debug), inventory, stamina, panels, results.
- [x] F1 debug overlay (answers, monster state), F2 skip phase, F3 freeze the Hollow, F4 teleport to your house.

**Tests**

- [x] Altar order logic, puzzle answer checks, phase timeline maths.

**Done when:** the user plays a full solo match in the greybox (read letter, find house, solve puzzle, carry part, place it, chant, cross, results) and can also lose to the Hollow.

## M1.5 — Look pass (interim, ahead of M7)

Replace greybox art with real free assets so playtests read as a damp, abandoned 1970s village at night. Rules for the whole pass: no changes to gameplay, layout, the `MatchPlan` or tuning numbers; every existing test stays green; art enters only through builders and editor scripts (no hand-written YAML, no `.meta` edits); gameplay colliders and interaction volumes stay exactly as they are. Commit after each green step.

**1. Plan**

- [x] This section, approved by the user before any downloads. Decisions (2026-10-22): remove HDRP and Terrain Tools, keep Shader Graph; import Essential Footsteps, Saloon Interior and Terrain Textures Free (only the files the builders use); leave the HS_MP music pack out (DESIGN: only in-world music).

**2. CC0 assets (Poly Haven, ambientCG)**

- [x] `tools/third-party.json`: one entry per chosen asset (id, source, URL, licence, what it's for). `tools/fetch-assets.ps1` downloads exactly those at 2K into `Assets/ThirdParty/PolyHaven/<id>/` and `Assets/ThirdParty/AmbientCG/<id>/`, so the set can be re-fetched.
- [x] Pick each asset from contact sheets of the APIs' preview images (damp, weathered, rural). Texture sets: weathered plaster, old timber planks, rotten floorboards, mossy roof shingles or thatch, dry-stone wall, muddy dirt road, cobblestone, forest-floor ground, rusty metal, soot/stone hearth, old fabric, plus bark. Two 2K night/overcast HDRIs.
- [x] Models (FBX mesh + 2K JPG maps, no EXR; small props at 1K): wooden chair, rocking chair, table, wardrobe/cabinet, bed, shelves, barrels, crates, lantern, candles, books, clocks, buckets, tools, dead trunks and stumps, rocks. Poly Haven has no wooden fence and its trees are 50–650 MB each, so fences and dead trees are built from textured primitives instead.
- [x] `ThirdPartyImport` editor script (an AssetPostprocessor): normal maps typed as Normal, mask maps linear, max size 2048; packed AO/rough/metal maps swizzled at import into URP Lit's metallic/occlusion/smoothness layout (no repacked files). `BuildModelMaterials` gives each model material a URP Lit asset filled from its own maps and remaps the FBX onto it.
- [x] EditMode test: every third-party texture is ≤ 2048 px and has the right import type; every model material is a remapped URP Lit asset with colour and normal maps.

**3. Manual downloads (`C:\dev\Hollowmere\AssetDownloads`)**

- [x] Import only the Asset Store packs the user picks, and only the files the builders use; everything else stays out of the project. `tools/import-assetstore.ps1` reads `tools/assetstore-imports.json` and extracts just the selected items from each `.unitypackage` into `Assets/ThirdParty/AssetStore/<pack>/`, without the vendor's metas, materials or prefabs: Essential Footsteps (wood, concrete, dirt and leaves walks 1–8 and landings), Terrain Textures Free (Grass 06, Grass 09, Leaves 02; HDRP mask maps converted to arm), and 13 Saloon Interior Megascans props (cart wheel, chest, bucket, barrel, chair, shelf, oil lamp, horseshoe, picture frame, beam, jug, stool, table). Megascans textures are read through their materials: albedo tint and brightness baked into `_diff`, the roughness channel remapped to the material's range in `_arm`, all at most 2K. Their `_ConvexHulls` collision meshes are dropped at import. `AssetDownloads/Mixamo` is empty and there is no Fab folder, so nothing was imported there; the import rules already rig `Assets/ThirdParty/Mixamo` as Humanoid.
- [x] Sonniss: copy only the clips SoundBank needs plus ambience (wind, night forest, rain, house creaks, distant dogs and crows) into `Assets/Hollowmere/Audio`, never whole libraries. `tools/prepare-audio.ps1` cuts 29 clips listed in `tools/audio-sources.json` (bell strikes, ambience loops with crossfaded seams, creaks, knocks, thuds, chimes, gasp, Hollow breathing, chant drone): 57 MB of 48 kHz WAV. ffmpeg isn't installed, so the sources stay WAV and `AudioImportRules` compresses every clip to Vorbis on import (ambience streamed, one-shots mono). The sampler has no crows.

**4. Materials**

- [ ] `MaterialLibrary` ScriptableObject keyed by the builder's existing material names (HouseWall, InnerWall, Roof, Floor, Ceiling, Road, Ground, Paving, Stone, Wood, ChestWood, GateWood, BridgeWood, Metal, HearthStone, Chimney, ChapelWall, Trunk, Leaves, Door_*, ...): base, normal, metallic/smoothness and occlusion maps, tint and metres-per-tile. `BuilderKit.Mat()` resolves through it and falls back to today's flat colour when a texture is missing.
- [ ] World-space tiling: `BuilderKit.Box()` uses cached box meshes whose UVs are in metres (one generated mesh asset per distinct size), so a 1 m patch of wall looks the same on a 12 m wall as on a 3 m one. Box colliders stay exactly as they are.

**5. Props and dressing**

- [ ] Swap primitives for models through the `Visual`/`Shell` slots where a good model exists: wardrobe, tables, hearth dressing, lantern and candles, chest, fences, dead trees, rocks. The primitive's collider stays (renderer hidden) and the model is fitted to its bounds, so collision, interaction and NavMesh don't change.
- [ ] Seeded, deterministic set dressing in rooms and streets (chairs, beds, shelves, books, buckets, tools, barrels, crates, debris, cobwebs if available), with no colliders, so it can't affect movement, interaction rays or the NavMesh bake.

**6. Lighting and atmosphere**

- [ ] `RenderSetup` editor script: Hollowmere's own URP asset and renderer for the PC quality level (Forward+, HDR, soft shadows at high resolution with 4 cascades, additional-light shadows for the flashlight and lamps, SSAO).
- [ ] Global Volume profile built by script: ACES tonemapping, colour grading, bloom on light sources, vignette, film grain. `PhaseLighting` also drives the post-processing per phase (gold day, draining dusk, dark readable night, thick Crossing fog).
- [ ] Warm lamp, candle and fire colours with a subtle cosmetic flicker (intensity only; a room light's on/off state, which the Hollow's sight uses, never changes). Flashlight: soft spot with a generated cookie.
- [ ] HDRIs for the overcast day sky and for reflections; night stays dark.

**7. Audio**

- [ ] `SoundLibrary` ScriptableObject; `SoundBank` uses its clips and falls back to the synthesised ones for anything missing.
- [ ] Looping ambience per phase and for indoors versus outdoors; footsteps per surface (wood, stone, dirt) chosen from what's under the step; door and creak variations. Every sound the Hollow can hear still goes through `NoiseSystem.Emit`.

**8. Repo hygiene**

- [ ] Git LFS for every binary (already set up by `.gitattributes`); nothing above 2K committed; `AssetDownloads` and raw packs never committed.
- [ ] `CREDITS.md` generated from `tools/third-party.json` plus the imported packs: every asset, its source URL and licence (CC0, Fab Standard, Mixamo, Sonniss, Unity Asset Store EULA).

**9. Verify**

- [ ] Rebuild the village; `check`, `test` (EditMode and PlayMode) green.
- [ ] `LookTools` renders day, dusk and night outside plus a house interior under runtime lighting; look at them and iterate.
- [ ] Screenshots and a short playtest script for the user.

**Done when:** the user's playtest says it looks like a place, and every M1 test still passes.
